9. Wyckoff Effort vs Result

Based on Wyckoff's principle: If volume is exceptional (>95th percentile) but the candle body is weak (<30% of range), institutions are distributing — selling while retail is buying.

✅ Real Breakout

Volume: XH (Extra High) + Candle: Big Green (Body = 75%) → Score += 2

⚠️ Distribution (Absorption)

Volume: XH (Extra High) + Candle: Doji (Body = 15%) → Score -= 2 (penalty!)

Dashboard shows: ⚠️ Absorption

10. Mini Backtester

Tracks the last 5 Confluence BUY signals and calculates win rate. Records Entry, SL, TP1 and monitors: did TP1 hit before SL?

ResultMeaningColorAction
≥ 60%Excellent performance🟢Enter with confidence
40-59%Average performance🟡Be cautious
< 40%Poor performance🔴Reduce size or wait

How it Works

Uses an Array Queue of 5 elements. On new signal: tracks until TP1 hit (✅ Win), SL hit (❌ Loss), or 50 bars timeout (= Loss). array.sum() = exact win count.

🇪🇬 EGX Daily Limit Protection

EGX has a ±10% daily limit. The indicator ensures SL and TP never exceed this limit — because if SL is beyond 10%, it simply won't execute!

★ Advanced Risk Manager V2

10 advanced tools that work automatically to protect capital and optimize performance — purpose-built for EGX.

🌡️ Portfolio Heat Map

Tracks total open risk across all positions. Above 6% = ⚠️ warning | Above 10% = 🚫 stop entering! Manual Open Trades Count input.

🚨 Drawdown Circuit Breaker

After 3 consecutive losses → blocks all BUY signals + zeros position size. Dashboard shows 🚫 BLOCKED. Protects from revenge trading.

📐 Volatility-Adjusted Exits

Adjusts TP/SL automatically based on ATR/SMA(ATR,50). High volatility = wider SL + closer TP. Low volatility = tighter SL + farther TP.

🔗 Correlation Guard

If stock correlation with index (EGX30) > 85% → reduce size by 30% to avoid overexposure to the same market direction.

⏰ Time-Based Risk Decay

Trailing stop tightens as trade ages: 0-10 bars = 2.0× | 11-25 = 1.5× | 26-50 = 1.0× | >50 = 0.7×

📊 Expectancy Calculator

Computes E(R) = (Win% × AvgWin) - (Loss% × AvgLoss) from real Mini Backtester data. If negative = system is losing!

Suggests better entry prices near AVWAP / EMA20 / Demand Zone to improve Risk:Reward ratio. Shows as 📍 Better and 📍 Best in dashboard.
